Terms and professional boundaries

Effective August 16, 2026.

This tool structures observations, hypotheses, project governance, and learning plans. It does not provide architecture, engineering, medical, legal, accessibility, code, regulatory, or other licensed professional services.

No compliance or outcome determination

Scores are prompts for inquiry, not certifications. A score or generated packet does not establish code or accessibility compliance, safety, fitness for purpose, medical benefit, causation, or an evidence-based standard of care.

Professional responsibility

Users remain responsible for qualified professional review, applicable approvals, source verification, participation by affected communities, and decisions made from the output. Do not rely on the workbench for emergency or safety-critical decisions.

Evidence and measurement

Evidence applicability depends on population, context, intervention, outcome, implementation, and currentness. Measurement should minimize surveillance and respect consent, privacy, accessibility, equity, retention, and organizational requirements.

Availability

The tool is provided as-is and may change. Maintain your own exports of important work.
