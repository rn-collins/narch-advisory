Privacy

Effective August 16, 2026.

The workbench stores project entries in your browser's local storage. NARCH does not receive those entries unless you deliberately share an exported file or contact us.

Vercel may process ordinary request, security, performance, and aggregate analytics data needed to deliver and protect the site. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights; it does not use advertising cookies.

Use Reset to remove the locally stored project, or clear site data in your browser. Exported files are under your control. Do not enter sensitive personal, health, or confidential project information unless your organization has approved local-browser storage for that use.

Contact
