About

Six Hundred Studies. One Usable Framework.

Six hundred studies confirm what architects already sense: the built environment is not a neutral backdrop for human activity. It regulates stress, cognition, healing, productivity, and social cohesion at the biological level. NARCH makes that evidence usable.

The Biophilic Evidence Audit grounds specific design interventions in peer-reviewed neuroscience and environmental psychology research. The output is a document your team can put in front of a client, a grant committee, or a public planning board — with citations.

NARCH operates at the intersection of NSAG's M5 (Biophilic Civic Infrastructure), M7 (Conscious Cities), and M10 (Healthcare Built Environment) — three modules that represent a combined governance opportunity across municipal planning, healthcare architecture, and civic design.
