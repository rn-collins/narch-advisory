Measurement plan

Pair baseline, outcome, feedback, and unintended-effect measures. Decide in advance what would change the decision.

Avoid surveillance by default. Use the least intrusive measure that answers the question, minimize personal data, obtain appropriate consent, and define retention and access before collection.
